PROMOTIONAL TEXT (max 170)
See how likely each outcome is, with the uncertainty drawn, not hidden. Forecasts refresh daily and again shortly before tip-off.

DESCRIPTION
PickPulse X turns game results into clear probabilities for upcoming basketball games.

FOR EVERY GAME
- Each team's chance to win
- The likely score, total and margin, with an 80% range that shows how uncertain a result really is
- First half and first quarter outlooks
- Player stat ranges once rosters and recent games are known
- A short written take on what is driving the forecast

THRESHOLD EXPLORER
Pick any total or margin you like and see the chance a game finishes above it, below it or exactly on it. The thresholds are yours; the chances come straight from the forecast.

INJURY REPORTS, FACTORED IN
As a game gets close, injury reports are added and the numbers shift. You can see who is listed and how they are listed, right on the game page.

UPDATED EVERY DAY
Forecasts refresh daily with the latest results and schedules, with one last update shortly before each game starts.

SEE HOW IT PLAYED OUT
Past days show the forecast made before tip-off next to the final score, so you can judge for yourself.

THE BOARD
Every team's form at a glance: Elo, offense, defense and net rating, plus a style view of pace, shooting, turnovers, rebounding and free throws.

DESIGNED TO BE CLEAR
A calm, editorial design with light and dark appearance, built for quick reading.

ANALYSIS ONLY
PickPulse X is statistical analysis, not advice, and makes no guarantees. No accounts, no ads and no tracking.
