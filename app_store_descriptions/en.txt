PickPulse X: Basketball Stats

SUBTITLE (max 30)
Win Probability & Predictions

PROMOTIONAL TEXT (max 170; can be changed any time without a new review)
Basketball forecasts for every game this week: win probability, projected scores with ranges and injury reports. Refreshed daily and before tip-off.

DESCRIPTION
How likely is each basketball game to go either way? PickPulse X shows you, game by game, for the next 7 days: win probability, projected score and honest ranges, refreshed every day and again shortly before tip-off.

WHAT YOU GET FOR EVERY GAME
- Win probability for both teams
- Projected score, total and margin, with an 80% range so you can see how uncertain a result really is
- First half and first quarter outlooks
- Player stat ranges once rosters and recent games are known
- A short written take on what is driving the forecast, from team form and rest to home court and who is on the injury report

INJURY REPORTS, FACTORED IN
As a game gets close, injury reports are added and the numbers shift. See who is listed and how, right on the game page.

TEST YOUR OWN NUMBERS
Pick any total or margin and see the chance a game finishes above it, below it or exactly on it. The thresholds are yours; the chances come straight from the forecast.

SEE HOW IT PLAYED OUT
Past days show the forecast made before tip-off next to the final score, so you can judge the numbers for yourself.

EVERY TEAM AT A GLANCE
The Board ranks every team by rating and form: Elo, offense, defense and net rating, plus a style view of pace, shooting, turnovers, rebounding and free throws.

MADE TO BE READ FAST
A calm, editorial design with light and dark appearance. Open it, scan the day, dive into any game.

ANALYSIS ONLY
PickPulse X is statistical analysis, not advice, and makes no guarantees. No accounts, no ads, no tracking.
