Privacy Policy

PickPulse X does not collect personal data. This page explains exactly what the app does on your device and over the network.

Last updated October 6, 2026

1. Who we are

PickPulse X ("we", "us", "our") publishes the PickPulse X app for iPhone. For any privacy question, write to support@norfa.fun.

2. Data we collect

None. PickPulse X has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising identifiers and no tracking. It does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or any other personal information, and it contains no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

3. What stays on your device

  • A single setting that records that you have read the welcome notice, so it is not shown every time you open the app.
  • Your appearance choice (system, light or dark) while the app is open.

These never leave your device. Deleting the app removes them.

4. Network use

When you open the app, it downloads one public file of forecasts from our static host (served by Cloudflare). Like any web request, this exposes standard technical information to the host, such as your IP address, the time, and basic request details. The host may keep standard server logs for security and reliability. We do not attach this to any identity, we do not combine it with other data, and the app sends no information about you in the request. If the download fails, the app falls back to a forecast file bundled inside it.

5. Third parties

We use Cloudflare to host and deliver the forecast file, and Apple distributes the app through the App Store. Cloudflare processes the technical details of each download request (described in section 4) under its own privacy terms, only to deliver the file and keep the service secure. We do not share any user data with any other third party, and the app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs. We do not sell, rent or share personal information, because we do not collect any.

6. Retention and deletion

We do not retain personal data about you. The only information tied to your use of the app that exists anywhere is what stays on your device (section 3), plus the short-lived request logs described in section 4, which are kept by the host under its standard practice and are not linked to you. Deleting the app removes everything stored on your device, and there is nothing for us to delete on our side. If you think we hold data about you, write to us and we will look into it.

7. Consent and your choices

The app does not request any permissions and does not track you, so there is no collection that needs your consent and nothing to withdraw. You can stop all network use at any time by turning off the connection or deleting the app. Because we hold no personal data, there is nothing to access, correct or delete.

8. Children and security

The app is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone, including children. The forecast file is downloaded over an encrypted HTTPS connection, and the app is read-only: it cannot send data to us.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the new version here and update the date above.

10. Contact
